# Next art milestone: authored courtyard assets

Status: approved reference choices and proposed implementation brief, September 10, 2026. This refines the next visual milestone; it does not rewrite universe canon or certify that the next art pass is implemented.

Dan selected elements of **Death's Door**, **DREDGE** and **For the King**. Our proposed interpretation is clear silhouettes and considered framing; restrained colors and atmosphere; and appealing proportions with economical geometry. These references guide original AFTERMARKET art. No claim is made that these games used the packs below.

Keep AFTERMARKET's cream ceramic architecture, teal machines, brass accents, botanical overgrowth, humorous ancient technology and fixed elevated gameplay view. Moss remains an earlier visual aspiration, not the acceptance gate for this first game.

## First scene

One service-machine corner: a small floor, doorway, two wall sections, two plant types, rocks, the existing traveler for scale, and one technology prop. Begin with authored geometry. Prior procedural passes established working gameplay and an export workflow, but Dan judged their visual fidelity insufficient. Improve geometry selection, palette, lighting, composition and motion deliberately rather than adding more simple shapes.

## Proposed art rules

1. Use cream and muted green for large surfaces; reserve teal and brass for useful technology and focal details.
2. Keep one clear route and one primary focal object in the test composition.
3. Compare assets at the gameplay camera distance; close Blender renders alone are insufficient.
4. Use broad readable forms, selective edge rounding and restrained surface detail. Match scale, texture density and material response across sources.
5. Use lighting supported by the existing renderer and measure its cost. Do not assume volumetric effects or baked GI are available or already configured.

## Definition of a reviewable result

A real Godot test scene, original source and license records, successful applicable project checks, and a gameplay screenshot inspected for route clarity, player visibility, correct materials and composition. Distinguish concept art, Blender previews, Godot captures and Windows performance measurements. Local Codex prepares and verifies these; Dan chooses the visual direction and assesses play feel.
